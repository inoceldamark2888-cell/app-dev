# My Favorite Movie

Spider-Man: No Way Home is one of my favorite movies. It is an action and adventure movie about Peter Parker, who faces many challenges while trying to protect the people he loves.

### Why I Like This Movie

- The story is exciting.
- The action scenes are amazing.
- The characters are interesting.
- It teaches us about responsibility and sacrifice.

### My Favorite Character

**Spider-Man (Peter Parker)**

I like this character because he is brave, helpful, and always tries to do the right thing.
